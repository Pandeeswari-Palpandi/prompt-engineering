# Role

Act as an experienced professional **Chef and Nutrition-Focused Meal Planner**, specializing in healthy Indian and Tamil Nadu cuisine.

# Task

Create a **7-Day Unique Healthy Meal Plan** specifically designed for **gym members, fitness enthusiasts, and people trying to develop healthy eating habits**.

The meal plan should be practical, nutritious, affordable, and easy to prepare at home using commonly available ingredients in Tamil Nadu.

# Target Audience

- Gym members
- Fitness enthusiasts
- People following a healthy lifestyle
- Beginners who want simple homemade healthy meals
- People looking for balanced Tamil Nadu/Indian food options

# Regional Context

Design the meals considering:

- Tamil Nadu's typical hot and humid weather
- Traditional Tamil food habits
- Commonly available local ingredients
- Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food preferences
- Affordable and easily accessible ingredients
- Home-style cooking
- Foods suitable for pre/post-workout nutrition

# Meal Planning Requirements

Create meals for all **7 days**, with:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner

Each day should contain **different and interesting meal combinations**. Avoid repeating the same meal frequently.

Prioritize:

- High-protein foods
- Complex carbohydrates
- Fiber-rich foods
- Healthy fats
- Fresh vegetables
- Seasonal/local fruits
- Adequate hydration
- Balanced nutrition

Prefer healthier cooking methods such as:

- Steaming
- Boiling
- Grilling
- Air frying
- Pressure cooking
- Light sautéing

Avoid or minimize:

- Deep-fried foods
- Excessive sugar
- Highly processed foods
- Excessive refined carbohydrates
- Excessive oil
- Sugary beverages
- Highly processed snacks

# Ingredients

Use ingredients that are commonly available in a typical Tamil Nadu household, such as:

- Rice
- Brown rice
- Millets
- Ragi
- Oats
- Idli/Dosa batter
- Dal
- Sambar
- Vegetables
- Eggs
- Chicken
- Fish
- Paneer
- Curd
- Milk
- Sprouts
- Groundnuts
- Nuts and seeds
- Fruits
- Coconut
- Spices and herbs

Do not require expensive, imported, or difficult-to-find ingredients unless absolutely necessary.

# Nutrition Requirements

For every meal, provide an approximate:

- Calories
- Protein in grams

Keep the meals suitable for fitness-oriented people and aim for a good protein intake throughout the day.

Nutrition values can be approximate estimates rather than medically precise values.

# Preparation Requirements

For every meal, provide:

1. **Meal Name**
2. **Required Ingredients**
3. **Simple Method of Preparation**
4. **Approximate Calories**
5. **Approximate Protein (g)**

Preparation instructions should be:

- Short
- Clear
- Beginner-friendly
- Easy to follow
- Suitable for home cooking

# Output Format

Provide the complete result in a **Markdown table** with the following columns:

| Day | Meal | Meal Name | Required Ingredients | Method of Preparation | Calories | Protein (g) |
| --- | ---- | --------- | -------------------- | --------------------- | -------- | ----------- |

Where `Meal` should be one of:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner

# Important Guidelines

- Create exactly **7 days × 3 meals = 21 meals**.
- Make the meals **unique and varied**.
- Include a mix of traditional Tamil Nadu foods and modern healthy meal options.
- Ensure the meals are realistic for a normal household kitchen.
- Keep preparation time reasonably short.
- Balance carbohydrates, protein, healthy fats, vegetables, and fiber.
- Include both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options across the week.
- Use practical portion sizes.
- Do not make extreme calorie restrictions.
- Avoid making medical or disease-specific dietary claims.
- Mention that calorie and protein values are approximate.

# Final Output

Start with the title:

**7 Days Unique Healthy Meal Plan – Tamil Nadu Style**

Then provide the complete 7-day meal plan in the requested Markdown table format.

At the end, provide a short section:

### Healthy Habits for the Week

Include 5–7 practical tips covering:

- Water intake
- Portion control
- Protein intake
- Fruits and vegetables
- Workout nutrition
- Sleep
- Avoiding processed foods

Keep the overall meal plan **simple, healthy, affordable, practical, and suitable for Tamil Nadu food habits and weather**.
